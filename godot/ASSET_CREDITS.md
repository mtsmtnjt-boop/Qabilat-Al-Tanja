# 3D asset credits

- `quaternius-tank.glb` and `quaternius-guard-tower.glb`: Quaternius models fetched from the OpenTop asset mirror.
- `b_small.glb`, `b_medium.glb`, `b_large.glb`, `tree1.glb`, `tree2.glb`, `tree3.glb`: Quaternius models from the Skyline Run public asset bundle. Its credits identify the building and nature models as CC0 1.0.

Sources:
- https://github.com/dhb118/opentop
- https://github.com/anshaneja5/skyline-run/blob/main/CREDITS.md

The Godot workflow downloads the GLB files during the build, imports them, then exports the Android package. This is an asset-integration milestone, not a claim that the full game is complete.
